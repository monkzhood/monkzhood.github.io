HOW IT WORKS?

01

Connect

Connect any injected wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase…) on Robinhood Chain — ID 4663. Don't have the network yet? Hit SWITCH in the banner and it's added to your wallet in one click.

02

Claim free

Every wallet gets 1 free mint — no ETH needed, only gas — while the 5,000 free allotment lasts. One free mint per block network-wide; wait a few seconds if the button shows a cooldown.

03

Paid mint

Mint more at 0.000069 ETH per token — up to 69 per transaction and 69 per wallet. ETH goes straight to the payout address; this page never holds funds.

04

Hold

Minted tokens land in your wallet. Keep them — view any time on the explorer or in your NFT wallet.

!

Why did my free mint revert?

Only one free mint per block is allowed. During busy moments someone may claim right before you — the tx reverts and you only lose a little gas. Wait a few seconds and try again when the button stops saying COOLING.

?

Can I mint 69 NFTs in a single paid transaction?

Yes. The paid mint allows up to 69 tokens per transaction, as long as you stay within your wallet limit of 69 paid mints and there are enough tokens left. Use the quantity stepper or Max, check the total price, then confirm — all 69 will be minted to your wallet in one transaction.

?

Why is free mint limited to 1 NFT per block?

Without this limit, bots could mint hundreds or thousands of NFTs in a single block or transaction, clearing the free allotment before real users get a chance. Allowing only one free mint per block keeps the free claim fair for everyone.
